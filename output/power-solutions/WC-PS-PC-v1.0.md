## WakeCap Power 30 (WP-30)

### 80W Solar | 384Wh LiFePO4 | Off-Grid Power System

[IMAGE: WP-30 complete system — compact pole-mounted unit showing the main controller box with integrated battery arm, two 40W solar panels in a butterfly configuration, and mounting clamp on a steel pole, photographed at a slight angle from below against a clear sky background, with LED indicators visible on the front panel]

The WakeCap Power 30 is an ultra-compact, pole-mounted solar power system designed for lightweight remote equipment. Featuring an integrated MPPT controller with BMS, LiFePO4 battery technology, and IP66-rated enclosure, the WP-30 delivers reliable 12V DC power for loads up to 8W in harsh desert and coastal environments. Its all-in-one design enables rapid installation on existing poles without additional infrastructure.

**Key Features:**
- Integrated MPPT controller with >95% efficiency and built-in BMS
- LiFePO4 battery (2P4S configuration) with 2000–5000 cycle life
- IP66 dust-tight and water-jet resistant enclosure
- LED status indicators for charging, output, and battery state
- Optional RS485 interface for remote monitoring
- Pole mount / wall mount installation (60–150mm pole diameter)

## WakeCap Power 60 (WP-60)

### 100W Solar | 768Wh LiFePO4 | Off-Grid Power System

[IMAGE: WP-60 complete system — modular pole-mounted unit showing the battery enclosure box, separate MPPT charge controller, two 50W solar panels with adjustable tilt bracket, and galvanized mounting structure attached to a steel pole, photographed from a three-quarter front angle showing the aviation-style waterproof connectors and DC output cable]

The WakeCap Power 60 is a modular pole-mounted solar power system for medium-demand remote equipment. With a standalone 10A MPPT controller, 60Ah LiFePO4 battery, and aviation-style waterproof connectors, the WP-60 delivers reliable 12V DC power for loads up to 15W. Its adjustable solar panel tilt (0–30°) and N-Type A+ Grade cells provide superior low-light performance for year-round operation.

**Key Features:**
- Standalone 10A MPPT charge controller with 96.5% efficiency
- 60Ah LiFePO4 battery with integrated BMS and full protection suite
- N-Type A+ Grade monocrystalline panels with >22% efficiency
- Aviation-style waterproof connectors for reliable field connections
- Adjustable panel angle (0–30°) for seasonal optimization
- Ultra-low controller self-consumption (0.06W)

## WakeCap Power 400 (WP-400T)

### 450W Solar | 4800Wh GEL | Trailer-Mounted Power Station

[IMAGE: WP-400T trailer-mounted power station — single-axle trailer with three 150W solar panels deployed in a tilted array, 6.5m pneumatic telescoping mast fully extended with equipment cabinet at the top, manual outriggers deployed for stability, ball hitch visible at front, and anti-oxidation powder-coated chassis, photographed from a three-quarter front angle in an open desert site setting with clear sky]

The WakeCap Power 400 is a mobile, trailer-mounted solar power station designed for temporary and semi-permanent deployments. Featuring a 6.5m pneumatic telescoping mast, 400Ah GEL deep-cycle battery bank, and integrated equipment cabinet with thermal management, the WP-400T delivers 12V DC power for loads up to 100W. **Key Features:**
- 6.5m pneumatic telescoping mast (5 sections, aluminum alloy)

- 30A MPPT charge controller
- Integrated equipment cabinet with electric fan cooling
- Exchange board for data and power distribution
- Single-axle US-standard trailer with ball hitch and manual outriggers
- Anti-oxidation electrostatic powder coat finish

## WakeCap Power 900 (WP-900T)

### 1305W Solar | 21600Wh Lead Acid | Heavy-Duty Trailer Power Station

[IMAGE: WP-900T heavy-duty trailer power station — large single-axle trailer with mechanical brake system, three 435W solar panels deployed in a wide tilted array, 9m manual telescoping mast tower fully extended with camera box mounted at the top, steel plate spring suspension visible, 15-inch wheels, powder-coated chassis, multiple cable passages visible on the equipment cabinet, photographed from a wide three-quarter angle in a construction site setting to convey scale]

The WakeCap Power 900 is the highest-capacity trailer power station in the WakeCap portfolio. Engineered for heavy-duty applications up to 350W continuous, the WP-900T features a 9m manual telescoping mast, 900Ah high-temperature lead-acid battery bank (24V), and an EPEVER 60A MPPT controller. With 21.6kWh of energy storage and 1305W of solar capacity, it supports demanding surveillance, communication, and monitoring installations in remote locations.

**Key Features:**
- 9m manual telescoping mast tower with CAT6 and power cable routing
- 6 x 150Ah high-temperature lead-acid batteries (3S2P, 24V system)
- EPEVER 60A MPPT charge controller with 98% efficiency
- 3 x 435W monocrystalline solar panels (1305W total)
- Camera box (412 x 412 x 416mm) at mast top
- Single-axle trailer with mechanical brake and steel plate spring suspension
- 100 km/h wind rating
- 8 units per 40ft shipping container
