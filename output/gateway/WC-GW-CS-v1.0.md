## 1. Regulatory Overview

The WakeCap Gateway is a wireless communications device incorporating a proprietary mesh radio transceiver and a cellular modem (LTE-M/NB-IoT). As a radio-frequency transmitting device, it is subject to radio equipment regulations in all markets where it is sold and deployed.

This document summarizes the regulatory certifications obtained or planned for the WakeCap Gateway. It covers radio type approval, safety certification, environmental compliance, electromagnetic compatibility (EMC), ingress protection, and country-specific market access approvals.

> **NOTE:** This document is a summary reference. The legally binding certificates and test reports are maintained separately and are available upon request from WakeCap regulatory affairs. Contact: [TBD].
