# Compliance Summary Template Instructions

This document defines the structure and content requirements for WakeCap Compliance Summaries.

---

## Purpose

Compliance Summaries provide a concise record of all certifications, regulatory approvals, and standards compliance for a WakeCap product. They serve as a quick reference for procurement, legal, and regulatory teams to verify that a product meets site and jurisdiction requirements.

---

## Target Audience

- Procurement and compliance officers
- HSE (Health, Safety, Environment) managers
- Legal and regulatory teams
- Site access and permit authorities
- Quality assurance engineers

---

## Document Characteristics

| Attribute | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Typical Length | 1-2 pages |
| Orientation | Portrait (A4 or Letter) |
| Primary Content | Certification tables, declarations, regulatory marks |
| Layout | Dense tabular format; minimal prose |
| Image Ratio | 10% visual (certification marks), 90% data |

## Content Rules

1. **Factual and precise** - Only list certifications actually obtained
2. **Include certificate numbers** - Every certification needs a traceable reference
3. **Validity dates** - Include expiration dates where applicable
4. **Use [TBD] for pending** - Mark certifications in progress clearly
5. **No marketing language** - This is a regulatory document
6. **Keep current** - Update when certifications change or expire

---

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

- [ ] All applicable certifications are listed
- [ ] Certificate numbers are included and correct
- [ ] Validity dates are current
- [ ] Environmental compliance (RoHS, REACH, WEEE) is addressed
- [ ] Ingress protection rating is documented
- [ ] Radio compliance is covered (if wireless product)
- [ ] Declaration of Conformity is referenced
- [ ] Export classification is included
- [ ] Related documents are cross-referenced
- [ ] Document ID and revision shown
- [ ] Fits on 1-2 pages
- [ ] No expired or revoked certifications listed as current
