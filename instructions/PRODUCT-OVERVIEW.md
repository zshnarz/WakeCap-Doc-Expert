# Product Overview Template Instructions

This document defines the structure and content requirements for WakeCap Product Overviews.

---

## Purpose

Product Overviews provide a marketing-oriented introduction to a WakeCap product. They build confidence and differentiation for non-technical decision makers such as project managers, safety directors, and procurement leads.

---

## Target Audience

**Version A (Marketing/Sales):**
- C-suite and project directors
- Safety managers
- Procurement and evaluation committees
- Partners and distributors

**Version B (Technical):**
- Technical evaluators
- IT and systems integration teams
- Solution architects

---

## Document Characteristics

| Attribute | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Typical Length | 2-4 pages |
| Orientation | Portrait (A4 or Letter) |
| Layout | More white space; short paragraphs; benefit blocks |
| Color Usage | Brand-forward accent color with accessible contrast |
| Hero Content | Use-case tiles, KPI callouts, proof points |
| Image Ratio | 40% visual, 60% text |

## Content Rules

1. **Benefits over features** - Lead with what the customer gains
2. **No unexplained acronyms** - Spell out everything or avoid altogether
3. **Quantify claims** - Use numbers, percentages, timeframes
4. **Short paragraphs** - Maximum 3-4 sentences per block
5. **Proof points** - Every differentiator needs supporting evidence
6. **Only provable claims** - No aspirational or unverified statements

---

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

- [ ] Problem-to-outcome narrative is compelling and factual
- [ ] System context diagram shows product in ecosystem
- [ ] Differentiators are supported by proof points
- [ ] Use cases cover construction and oil & gas
- [ ] Deployment story includes phases and timeline
- [ ] Security/reliability claims are provable
- [ ] No unexplained acronyms or jargon
- [ ] Call-to-action and next steps are clear
- [ ] Document ID and revision shown
- [ ] All [IMAGE] placeholders have descriptive text
- [ ] Version A uses benefit-focused language
- [ ] Version B includes more technical detail
