# Solution Brief Template Instructions

This document defines the structure and content requirements for WakeCap Solution Briefs.

---

## Purpose

Solution Briefs provide industry-specific positioning for WakeCap products. They frame a product or product combination around a specific industry challenge, showing how WakeCap technology delivers measurable outcomes in construction and oil & gas environments.

---

## Target Audience

**Version A (Marketing/Sales):**
- Industry decision makers (project directors, HSE managers)
- Sales team for vertical-specific conversations
- Channel partners targeting specific industries

**Version B (Technical):**
- Solution architects
- Technical consultants
- Integration partners

---

## Document Characteristics

| Attribute | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Typical Length | 2-4 pages |
| Orientation | Portrait (A4 or Letter) |
| Layout | Story-driven; problem-solution-outcome flow |
| Color Usage | Brand-forward; industry-relevant imagery |
| Hero Content | Industry scenario, outcome metrics, architecture |
| Image Ratio | 35% visual, 65% text |

## Content Rules

1. **Industry-specific language** - Use terminology familiar to the target vertical
2. **Outcome-focused** - Lead with results, not technology
3. **Quantify everything** - Use metrics, percentages, time savings
4. **Cite statistics** - Provide sources for industry data
5. **Solution, not product** - Frame around the problem being solved
6. **Keep it brief** - 2-4 pages maximum

---

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

- [ ] Industry challenge is specific and quantified
- [ ] Solution maps directly to the stated challenge
- [ ] Architecture diagram shows all components in context
- [ ] Outcomes are measurable and credible
- [ ] Deployment scenario includes realistic parameters
- [ ] Requirements and compatibility are clearly stated
- [ ] Statistics have source citations
- [ ] No unexplained acronyms or jargon
- [ ] Next steps include clear calls to action
- [ ] Document ID and revision shown
- [ ] Fits within 2-4 pages
