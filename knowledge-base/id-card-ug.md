1. **Wear your card** at all times while on-site — on a lanyard or clipped to your clothing.
2. **Charge it monthly** — plug in the USB cable, wait for a full charge, unplug.
3. **Report problems** to your supervisor — if your card isn't working, don't ignore it.

# What Your Card Does

The WakeCap Rechargeable ID Card is a wireless safety and attendance card assigned to you. Once charged and activated, it works automatically — you do not need to press any buttons or open any apps.

**Your card tracks:**

- Your **location** on the project site (which zone you are in)
- Your **attendance** (time in, time out, total time on-site)
- **Safety events** such as potential falls (free fall detection)

# Wearing Your Card

Wear the card **at all times** while you are on the project site.

**Recommended wearing methods:**

- **Lanyard** — hang the card around your neck, with the printed side facing outward.
- **Card holder clip** — attach the clip to your clothing at chest or waist level.

> **NOTICE**
> Do not share or swap your card with another worker. Each card is linked to the assigned person — sharing it causes incorrect attendance records and unreliable safety alerts for both workers.

# Charging Your Card

Charge your card **once a month**. Make it a routine — for example, charge on the first working day of each month so you never run out of battery mid-shift.

> **NOTICE**
> Do not wait until the card is completely dead. A fully discharged card takes longer to come back online and may miss attendance records until it reconnects.

## Before You Charge

> **WARNING**
> Ensure your hands are dry before handling the USB cable and charger. Do not charge the card near water, wet surfaces, or in damp environments. Inspect the card for cracks, swelling, or visible battery bulge — do not charge a damaged card. Use only the provided USB cable or a compatible replacement. Do not use frayed, damaged, or exposed cables.

## Charging Steps

1. Connect the USB charging cable to the port on the bottom edge of the card.
2. Plug the other end into any USB power source — a wall adapter, computer USB port, or USB hub.
3. Check the LED indicator — [TBD] means charging in progress, [TBD] means fully charged.
4. Disconnect the cable when charging is complete. Do not leave the card connected to the charger indefinitely.

> **NOTICE**
> Do not charge cards in direct sunlight, near heaters, or in environments above [TBD] C. Excessive heat reduces battery lifespan and may damage the card.

## Battery Life

How long a single charge lasts depends on the card's configuration:

| Configuration | Battery Life Per Charge |
|---------------|------------------------|
| Standard (30-second updates) | Approximately 1 month |
| Extended (3-minute updates) | Approximately 3 months |

Most sites use the **standard configuration** with a monthly recharge cycle.

# If Something Goes Wrong

Follow these steps in order. Stop as soon as the issue is resolved.

## Step 1 — Check the LED

Press the power button on the card. Do you see a green blink?

- **Yes** — The card has power. Go to Step 3.
- **No** — The battery is dead. Go to Step 2.

## Step 2 — Charge the Card

Connect the card to a USB charger for at least **30 minutes**. Then press the power button again.

- **LED blinks** — Go to Step 3.
- **Still no response** — The card may be defective. Report it to your supervisor for replacement.

## Step 3 — Reboot the Card

Press and release the power button. Wait **2 to 5 minutes** for the card to reconnect.

- **Card appears online** — Problem solved.
- **Still offline** — Go to Step 4.

## Step 4 — Contact Your Supervisor

Provide your supervisor with:

- Your **name** and **employee ID**
- The **card's device ID** (if known)
- **What you tried** (Steps 1–3 above)
- **Where on-site** you were when the problem occurred

## Lost or Damaged Card

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Card lost | Report to your supervisor immediately. They will deactivate the card and issue a replacement. |
| Card cracked, bent, or swollen | Stop using it. Do not charge it. Hand it to your supervisor for safe disposal and replacement. |
| Card dropped in water | Remove it from water, dry it off, and report to your supervisor. Do not attempt to charge a wet card. |

## False Fall Alerts

The card has a built-in fall detection sensor. Because the card hangs freely on a lanyard, it may occasionally trigger false alerts — for example, if the card swings sharply or is dropped.

If you receive a false fall alert, report it to your supervisor. The sensitivity can be adjusted.

# Safety Information

> **WARNING**
> Do not attempt to open the card housing. The card contains a sealed lithium polymer battery that is not user-serviceable. Opening the card may cause battery damage, swelling, or fire.

> **CAUTION**
> Do not puncture, crush, bend excessively, or expose the card to extreme heat (above 60 C). Damaged batteries may swell, leak, or ignite.

> **NOTICE**
> Dispose of damaged or end-of-life cards through your site's electronic waste disposal process. Do not discard in general waste. The card contains a lithium battery that must be handled according to local regulations.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is the WakeCap ID Card?**

A rechargeable wireless card that automatically records your presence on-site, tracks which zones you enter, and can detect potential fall incidents. It works without any action from you — just wear it.

**Do I need to press any buttons or open any apps?**

No. The card works automatically once charged and configured. You do not interact with it during normal use.

**Is my personal information stored on the card?**

Your personal information (name, employee details) is stored securely on the WakeCap Portal. The card itself only stores a device ID used by the system.

**Who configures the card?**

Your site administrator configures the card before distributing it to you. You do not need to configure anything yourself.

## Charging and Battery

**How often do I need to charge?**

Once a month. Pick a fixed day each month and make it a habit.

**How long does a full charge take?**

[TBD]. Leave the card connected until the LED indicates a full charge.

**What happens if my card battery dies?**

Your attendance will not be recorded and safety monitoring will pause for your card. Charge the card as soon as possible, reboot it by pressing the power button, and wait a few minutes for it to reconnect.

**Can I use any USB cable?**

Use the provided cable or a compatible USB cable that fits the card's port. Do not force a connector that does not match.

**The card feels warm while charging. Is that normal?**

Slight warmth is normal. If the card becomes hot to the touch, disconnect it immediately, move it away from heat sources, and report it to your supervisor.

## Daily Use

**Is the card waterproof?**

The card is designed for outdoor construction environments and can handle light rain and dust. Avoid submerging it in water.

**Can I put stickers on the card or write on it?**

Do not cover the center area or the LED indicator. Your employee details are printed by the site team using a specialized printer.

**What should I do if I lose my card?**

Report the loss to your supervisor immediately. They will deactivate the lost card and issue you a replacement.

**What should I do if my card is cracked, bent, or swollen?**

Stop using it immediately and do not attempt to charge it. Hand it to your supervisor for safe disposal.

**I see multiple detections in a short time on my record. Is this normal?**

Occasional rapid detections can happen at zone boundaries. If it occurs frequently, report it to your supervisor.

## Safety

**Does the card detect falls?**

Yes. The card has a built-in fall detection sensor. Because the card hangs on a lanyard, it may occasionally trigger false alerts when the card swings sharply or is dropped. Report any false alerts to your supervisor.

**How do I dispose of a card that no longer works?**

Do not throw it in general waste. The card contains a lithium polymer battery and must be disposed of through your site's electronic waste process.
